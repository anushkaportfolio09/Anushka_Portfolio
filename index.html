01 — Capture

"Every user is a person."

Your face. A single human being behind every click, every frustration, every moment of joy on a screen.

02 — Empathy

"I don't design for screens.
I design for people."

One face becomes many. Different ages, contexts, environments. Each one a person with a life outside your product.

03 — Research

"I listen before I solve."

Pain points. Needs. Goals. The best insights come not from data alone — but from sitting with people and really hearing them.

04 — Define

"Clarity comes from finding the real problem."

Messy research collapses into sharp insight. The noise clears. The problem becomes obvious — and so does the direction.

05 — Design

"Design is finding the clearest path forward."

From empathy to interface. The face becomes a flow, a wireframe, a system. Human-centered from start to pixel.

06 — Impact

"Behind every interface is a human experience."

That face? Still there. Now it moves through your design with ease, with clarity, with dignity. That's the work.
